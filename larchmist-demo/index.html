01 — The rooms

Three rooms, three kinds of warm.

Every room is lined with untreated cedar and heated by wood we split on site. Pick one, or move between all three during your two hours.

Hottest

The Ember Room

A small, dark room around a crackling stove. Pour water on the stones and feel the soft wave of steam.

03 — The ritual

Hot, cold, rest. Then again.

Most guests do three rounds. Your body will tell you when you are done.

112–15 min

Heat

Sit, breathe slow and let the warmth sink in. Leave when you feel ready, not when the clock says.

230–90 sec

Cold

Down the ladder into the lake, or the cold bucket if you are new. Short and calm is plenty.

315–20 min

Rest

Wrap up, sip tea, lie still. This is the part people remember all week.
