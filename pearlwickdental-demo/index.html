04 — Questions

Good to know

I'm really nervous. Is that okay?+

More than okay. Tell us when you book and we'll add extra time, explain each step first and stop whenever you raise a hand.

Can I pay monthly?+

Yes. Our care plan covers two check-ups and two cleans a year for £16 a month, and aligners can be spread over twelve months.

Do you see children?+

We do, from their very first tooth. Kids' visits are short and playful, and there's always a sticker at the end.

What if I have a toothache today?+

We keep two same-day slots free every morning for pain. Send us a message and we'll fit you in.
