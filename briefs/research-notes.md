# Short-form editing research notes

Running list of concrete, sourced takeaways. Techniques and principles only, never a copied video, script, sequence, music or brand.

## 2026-10-04 · focus: hooks and story-led retention (bobblefinswim-demo)

- **The keep-or-skip call happens in ~1.7 s on TikTok (~2.1 s on Reels).** Frame 1 has to show tension plus visual proof of it: no intro, no logo, no slow fade-in. ([ClipSpeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/hook-first-second-video-retention.html), [GoFaceless](https://www.gofaceless.ai/blog/mastering-first-impressions-two-second-rule))
- **Start mid-action or mid-sentence.** Dropping the viewer into something already happening opens a curiosity gap the brain wants closed. ([GoFaceless: how to write a video hook](https://www.gofaceless.ai/blog/how-to-write-a-video-hook))
- **POV works when it gives the viewer a role, a situation and a complication inside 3–5 s**, with captions written as direct address ("you open the next tab…"). An open question at 0–2 s plus the payoff at the end drives re-watches. ([Opus Clip: TikTok hooks 2026](https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-hooks-hijacking-algorithm-2026), [Socialkit storytelling guide](https://socialk.it/en/blog/tiktok-storytelling-guide))
- **Type should carry the message, not decorate it.** Most short-form is watched muted, and word-level animated captions show up on the large majority of high performers. Purposeful motion (words that slide or scale on the beat) beats look-at-me effects. ([Influencers Time: kinetic typography](https://www.influencers-time.com/kinetic-typography-boost-video-retention-on-tiktok-and-reels/), [ClipSpeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/hook-first-second-video-retention.html))
- **A beat of silence before a reveal often hits harder than another riser**, and most edits improve by deleting 30–50% of their sound effects so the rest only mark real visual moments. ([NemoVideo SFX workflow](https://www.nemovideo.com/blog/ai-sound-effect-generator-short-videos))

## 2026-10-04 · focus: editing to a vocal song + 3D camera depth (chainwrencycles-demo)

- **Put the song's peak on the story's peak.** A chorus that lands on an empty, low-information shot wastes the strongest moment in the track. Map the song's sections first, then place the reveal or payoff where the chorus or main vocal hook hits. ([DaVinci Resolve guide, via search summary](https://davinciresolve21.com/blog/how-to-edit-video-to-music-without-cutting-on-beat))
- **Cut on phrases and lyric changes, not every beat.** Downbeats and phrase boundaries make the cleanest transitions; lyric changes, swells and pauses are also valid cut points. "Let the music carry the energy, but let the story choose the cut." ([same source](https://davinciresolve21.com/blog/how-to-edit-video-to-music-without-cutting-on-beat), [Foxi Music: editing music for video 2026](https://www.foximusic.com/blog/es/?p=597))
- **Sync text reveals and zooms to musical hits** (chorus entry, a horn stab, a drum fill). It reads as intentional and keeps attention through the middle. ([ShortGenius: how to create video with music](https://shortgenius.com/blog/how-to-create-video-with-music), [CapCut beat-detection workflow](https://www.capcut.com/create/beat-detection-workflow-goal-compilation-edits))
- **Kinetic type should surface the one key word per line, with few animated elements at once.** Readable letters plus purposeful motion beat effects; continuous movement is fine as long as it never gets fast enough to disorient. ([Convince & Convert: what is kinetic typography](https://www.convinceandconvert.com/?p=153682), [moonb: kinetic typography examples](https://www.moonb.io/blog/kinetic-typography))
- **Depth comes from layers moving at different speeds**: foreground faster than background, plus a camera that moves through Z and a shallow depth of field on the far layers. A slow orbit or dolly that settles is what reads as "3D", not a tilt alone. ([Adobe: animate a picture in After Effects](https://www.adobe.com/learn/after-effects/web/animate-picture), [Tuts+: realistic camera move with parallax](https://photography.tutsplus.com/tutorials/create-a-realistic-camera-move-with-parallax--ae-453))
