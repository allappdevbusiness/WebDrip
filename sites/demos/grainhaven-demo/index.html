Good questions

Before you send a roll

Can I post my film from anywhere?+

Yes. Order a free prepaid label online, pop your rolls in a padded envelope and send them our way. We email you the moment they arrive.

What if my camera had a problem?+

If a roll comes out blank or strange, we'll tell you what likely happened and won't charge for the scans.

Do you make prints too?+

We do. Pick your favourite frames from the download page and we'll print them on matte or gloss paper.

Is the darkroom good for beginners?+

Totally. Your first visit starts with a free walk-through, and someone from the lab is always nearby.
