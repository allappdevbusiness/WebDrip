Exhibit 02 · How it plays

Four steps to the exit.

No experience needed. Your host watches every move and slides in a hint the moment you need one.

Grab a time slot
  1. 01

    Pick a room

    Choose a story and a time. Book the whole room so it is only your crew inside.

  2. 02

    Meet your host

    A five-minute briefing, lockers for your bags, and one walkie-talkie for help.

  3. 03

    Search, solve, shout

    Open drawers, crack codes, argue about the clock. Teamwork does the rest.

  4. 04

    Get out, get the photo

    Beat the clock or not, you leave with a team photo and your time on the wall.

Spotlight

Every key opens something.

Not always a door. Sometimes a drawer, a music box, or the next part of the story.

Ask about dates

Before you come

Good questions.

Are we actually locked in?+

Never. The real exit is always unlocked, and you can walk out any time. The lock you are chasing is part of the game.

Is it scary?+

Moody, not scary. No jump scares and no actors grabbing you, just low light, good music and a ticking clock.

What age can play?+

Ten and up with an adult in the room, fourteen and up on their own. The Chained Door is our favourite for families.

How early should we arrive?+

Fifteen minutes before your slot, so there is time for the briefing and a photo with the moth.
