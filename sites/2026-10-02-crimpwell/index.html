01 — The walls

Three zones. One for every mood.

Our setters reset one zone every Tuesday, so there's always something new by the weekend. Colours mark the problem, tags mark the grade.

The Slab

V0–V4

Gentle angles and big yellow feet. Where balance beats strength and first-timers find their feet.

54 problems · reset week 1

The Prism

V2–V6

Giant faceted volumes you can smear, pinch and press. Puzzles that reward a second look.

68 problems · reset week 2

V4–V9

03 — This week

Learn it, then send it.

Small groups, friendly coaches and everything you need provided. Book one session or come every week.

  1. Mon & Thu · 6:30pm · 90 min

    First Ascent intro

    Never climbed? Learn how to fall, read a problem and move your feet. Shoes included.

    £24 · max 8 people

  2. Saturday · 10am · 60 min

    Kids' crew (ages 6–12)

    Games, colour challenges and plenty of high fives on our low wall. Parents can climb too.

    £14 · per child

  3. Wednesday · 7pm · 75 min

    Technique lab

    Heel hooks, drop knees and quiet feet. Break through your grade plateau with a coach.

    £20 · V3 and up

  4. Friday · 6pm till late

    Reset social

    Try the week's freshest problems together, swap beta and stay for music on the mats.

    Free with any pass
