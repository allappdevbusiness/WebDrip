Flight day

One morning, start to finish

About three hours from hello to home, with breakfast still ahead of you.

  1. 05:30

    Meet at the field

    Coffee, a short safety chat and a first look at your balloon laid out on the grass.

  2. Fill the balloon

    Fans push in cool air, then the burner warms it until the balloon stands tall.

    05:50

  3. 06:12

    Lift off

    No jolt, no noise between burns. The field just drops away beneath your feet.

  4. Land and toast

    A soft landing in a farmer's field, a glass raised, and a story you will tell for years.

    07:15

  5. 08:30

    Back for breakfast

    The chase van drops you at your car with the whole day still ahead.
