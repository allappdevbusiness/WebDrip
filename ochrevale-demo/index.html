04 — Good to know

Before you pack.

How do we get there?+

We pick you up from the town square in our own car. The last 9 km are a sandy track, so leave the rental behind.

When is the best time to come?+

October to April for warm days and cold, clear nights. Summer is hot, but the pools and thick walls help a lot.

Is there Wi-Fi?+

In the library only. Most guests stop asking by day two.

Can we bring children?+

Guests aged 12 and up are welcome. Kids love the star nights.
