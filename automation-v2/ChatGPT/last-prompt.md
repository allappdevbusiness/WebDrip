## 7. Motion system
Use approximately 12–16 meaningful shots or reframings, connected by visual continuity. The nine story sections are not nine static slides.

- Entrances: gentle displacement, soft opacity and carefully masked text. Most settle within 0.6–1.0s.
- Camera: restrained pans and 3–8% push/pull changes across beauty shots. No repeated full-frame “breathing” scale.
- Depth: foreground floral layer, middle UI/photo window and paper background. Keep parallax shallow.
- Typography: animate by word or line, not flying individual characters. Body text stays still. Keep important phrases readable for around two seconds.
- Paper turn: one signature use, not every scene. Build a simple planar fold/occlusion and shadow; avoid rubber-sheet distortion.
- Rose line: thin and deliberate, used at three key story connections. Avoid decorating every border.
- Focus: blur foreground image layers modestly; never blur the whole UI while the viewer needs to read it.
- Color: retain pale paper and accurate flower colors; rose for emphasis. No gold/amber cast from the earlier edit.
- Avoid bouncing springs, sparkle showers, synthetic petals everywhere, fake lens flares and heart-shaped wipes.
- Keep actual brand logos intact, properly proportioned and supported by names. Use official assets or verified wordmarks; do not invent a Codex icon or imply endorsement.
