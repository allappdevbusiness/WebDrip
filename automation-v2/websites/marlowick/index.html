03 — How a fitting works

About 45 minutes, start to finish.

Book a time, come in as you are, and leave with everything measured and pinned.

  1. 0 min

    Tell us the occasion

    A wedding, a new job, a black-tie dinner. It decides what we bring out first.

  2. 5 min

    Try on two or three sizes

    We start with the jacket, because the shoulders are the one thing that can’t be changed later.

  3. 20 min

    Pin and chalk

    Sleeves, waist and trouser length are marked while you stand at the mirror.

  4. 35 min

    Pick the shirt and tie

    We lay out a few options next to the cloth so you can see them together.

  5. 3–5 days

    Collect it ready to wear

    Come back for a final try-on. If anything feels off, we fix it there and then.
