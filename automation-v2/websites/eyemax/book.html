Before you come in

  • Wear your contact lenses in

    For a contact lens check, wear your lenses for at least two hours before you arrive so we can see how they settle.

  • Skip the eye make-up for dry eye visits

    Mascara and liner sit on the gland openings we need to look at. Come with clean lids if you can.

  • Leave time for frames

    The exam takes 30 to 45 minutes. If you want to choose glasses on the same day, allow another 30 minutes.

  • Bringing a child

    Morning slots work best for under-fives. Bring their favourite toy, and do not worry if they do not know their letters yet.

What happens after your exam

From the moment you leave the consulting room to the point your new glasses feel like your own. Most people need just one more visit to collect them.

  1. 1

    Same day: your prescription

    You leave with a printed copy of your prescription and a short summary of what we found. If we took OCT or retinal images, we can email them to you that evening so you have your own record to compare next time.

  2. 2

    Days 1 to 7: your glasses are made

    Lenses are ordered the same afternoon. Single vision lenses usually arrive in three to five working days and varifocals or thin lenses in five to seven. We edge and glaze them in our own workshop and check every pair against your prescription before we text you.

  3. 3

    Collection: a proper fitting

    Collections take about fifteen minutes. We warm and shape the arms, set the nose pads and check that the optical centres line up with your pupils. For varifocals we check the reading zone with a book and a phone before you leave.

  4. 4

    Weeks 2 to 8: aftercare

    Most new prescriptions settle within a week or two. If anything still feels off, come back. Prescription rechecks within 30 days and varifocal adjustments within 60 days are free, and adjustments are free for as long as you own the frame.

Access, support and paying

Tell us when you book if you need anything set up before you arrive. You will not need to explain it again on the day.

  • Step-free and ground floor

    A level entrance from Fernhill Road, a wide front door and both consulting rooms, the fitting bench and the accessible toilet on the ground floor.

  • Hearing and sight support

    A hearing loop at the front desk and in room one. Large-print prescriptions and price lists on request, and we will read anything out for you.

  • Longer appointments

    If you would like more time, for example for a relative living with dementia or a child who needs breaks, ask for a double slot at no extra cost.

  • Ways to pay

    Cards, contactless, health savings and flexible spending cards. We give itemised receipts so you can claim from your vision insurance.

  • Spreading the cost

    Care plans spread exam costs across the year. For glasses over $300 we can split the payment across three months with no interest.

  • Home visits

    For patients who cannot leave home, Dr Okafor runs home visits on the first Friday of each month. Call us to check whether you qualify.

Booking policy, briefly

  • 24 hours’ notice

    Please let us know a day ahead if you cannot make it, so someone else can have the slot.

  • 10 minutes’ grace

    Running late? We can usually still see you within ten minutes. After that we offer the next free slot.

  • Second no-show fee

    A second missed appointment without notice carries a $25 fee. The first one never does.

  • Children and carers

    Under-16s are seen with a parent or carer in the room. Bring a favourite toy for the under-fives.
