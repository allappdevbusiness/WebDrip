# EyeMax: content research notes

This is a fictional concept brand: an independent optician and eyewear studio. These notes record facts and ideas only. No copy, names, price lists or layouts were taken from any real business.

## Exams and pricing
- At independent practices, a comprehensive exam typically costs $150–200 for new patients and $100–150 for returning ones. Chains charge about $45–100. Retinal imaging and visual fields are often extra, though some practices include an OCT scan as standard. ([bettercare](https://bettercare.com/costs/eye-exam-cost-without-insurance), [terra.do OCT guide](https://new.terra.do/oct-eye-test-price))
- An exam includes acuity, refraction, pupil response, eye pressure and screening for glaucoma, cataract and macular degeneration. Glasses and contacts are billed separately.
- How often: every 1–2 years for adults 18–64, yearly from 65 or with diabetes or a family history. Children aged 6–12 with myopia: twice a year. ([allaboutvision](https://allaboutvision.com/eye-care/eye-exam/questions-to-ask-eye-doctor), [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/life/thegoods/an-optometrist-answers-our-most-commonly-asked-eye-related-questions-1.4607030))
- Exams can also pick up general health signs (blood pressure, diabetes). Glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy and AMD can progress without symptoms.

## Lenses and glasses
- A full pair costs about $200–600. Single-vision lenses cost around $100. Progressives run up to $600 and more for premium designs. ([accruesavings](https://www.accruesavings.com/post/how-much-do-glasses-cost), [binder-optik](https://blog.binder-optik.de/en/ask-your-optician/progressive-glasses-costs))
- Add-ons: anti-reflective $30–95, photochromic about $67, blue-light filter about $50. ([fortlauderdale.gov plan doc](https://fortlauderdale.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/59264/637559727293700000))
- Frame sizes read as lens width–bridge–temple (for example 52-18-140, in mm). Temples are usually 135/140/145. The frame top shouldn't sit above the brows. ([Warby Parker learn](https://www.warbyparker.com/learn/eyeglass-frame-sizes-explained), [Specsavers AU size guide](https://www.specsavers.com.au/glasses/buyers-guide/frame-size))
- Acetate: warm colours, durable, needs heat adjustment and mild soap only. Titanium: light, hypoallergenic, more expensive. Care: two hands on and off, never on top of the head, never left in a hot car, rinse and dry with microfibre. ([Leightons](https://www.leightons.co.uk/blog/glasses-sunglasses/the-pros-and-cons-of-different-materials-used-for-glasses-frames), [Pair](https://paireyewear.com/blogs/news/acetate-vs-metal-frames))
- Prescription sunglasses: UV400, polarised for water and driving glare, available in single vision or varifocal. ([Airedale Opticians](https://airedaleopticians.co.uk/frames-lenses-and-glasses/sunglasses))

## Contact lenses
- A fitting measures corneal curvature and pupil size, then tries trial lenses. A teach session covers insertion and removal. Patients take trial lenses home for 7–14 days, with aftercare at 4–6 weeks. ([feelgoodcontacts](https://feelgoodcontacts.com/eye-care-hub/contact-lenses/fitting), [Specsavers aftercare](https://www.specsavers.co.uk/contact-lenses/aftercare))

## Kids, dry eye, repairs and policies
- Myopia-control spectacle lenses slow progression. Polycarbonate lenses suit sport. Some practices give free damage cover on kids' glasses. ([Myopia Profile](https://www.myopiaprofile.com/articles/stellest-lens-spectacles-sports))
- Dry eye: lid hygiene, warm compresses (masks about $35–45) and heat-and-expression treatments. IPL costs $300–800 per session across 2–4 sessions. Screens drop the blink rate from about 21 to 6–7 a minute. The 20-20-20 rule helps.
- Repairs: free adjustments, screws and nose pads are common for own customers. Elsewhere, about $10–30. Usually walk-in. ([Vision Express servicing](https://www.visionexpress.com/customer-services/free-lifetime-servicing))
- Varifocal non-adapt guarantees run 30–60 days, with a remake or a switch to single vision. Non-prescription returns are 30 days. ([Asda Opticians T&Cs](https://opticians.asda.com/instore-terms-and-conditions), [Le Petit Lunetier](https://lepetitlunetier.com/en-gb/pages/garantie))
- Booking: 24–48h cancellation notice, a 10–15 min late window, a no-show fee of $25–50. Saturdays are often 9–1 or 9–3. ([AAPC](https://www.aapc.com/codes/coding-newsletters/my-optometry-coding-alert/build-a-better-business-clearly-outline-practice-expectations-with-no-show-letters-and-fees-article))

## Trust and website features
- Patients trust independents for continuity (same optometrist each year), thoroughness and a personal relationship. ([Optometric Management](https://optometricmanagement.com/issues/2010/january/business-advisor/), [Bain](https://www.bain.com/insights/the-future-of-independent-optometry/))
- Strong optician sites offer online booking, shop by shape and width, frame filters, try-on or home try-on, a frame reserve, reviews and clear prices. ([Limely](https://www.limely.co.uk/blog/our-7-best-optician-websites), [iMatrix](https://imatrix.com/?p=33668))
