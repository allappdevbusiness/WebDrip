Lens materials and coatings, explained

Keep scrolling to move through the options. On a phone, swipe the cards.

  • 01

    Standard 1.5 plastic

    Clear optics and the lowest price. Best for low prescriptions up to about ±2.00.

  • 02

    Polycarbonate

    Very impact-resistant, so it is our default for children, sport and rimless frames.

  • 03

    Thin 1.6

    About 20 percent thinner than standard. A sensible upgrade from ±2.50 or so.

  • 04

    Ultra-thin 1.67

    For stronger prescriptions. Keeps the edge of the lens from showing past the frame.

  • 05

    Anti-reflective

    Cuts reflections from about 8 percent to under 1. Includes scratch and easy-clean layers.

  • 06

    Light-reactive

    Clear indoors, darkens outside in UV. Note that most car windscreens block the UV that triggers it.

  • 07

    Polarised

    Blocks glare bouncing off water and wet roads. Ideal for driving sunglasses and fishing.

  • 08

    Blue-light filter

    A faint tint that filters some blue light. Helpful for some people, not a fix for eye strain on its own.

Contact lenses, children and dry eyes

Three services that need more time than a standard exam, so each has its own clinic slot in the week.

Contact lenses, taught properly

A fitting starts with measurements of the curve and size of your cornea and your pupil size in different light. We choose a trial lens, check how it moves on the eye, and send you home with trial lenses for one to two weeks. Before you leave, you will put them in and take them out yourself, more than once, with someone watching.

  • Daily disposables, monthlies and toric lenses for astigmatism
  • Multifocal contact lenses for reading without glasses
  • Aftercare checks at 1 week and 4 to 6 weeks
  • Lens supply by post every 3 months, cancel any time

Kids' eyes

Children rarely complain that they cannot see, because they assume everyone sees the same way. We test from age three with pictures and shapes, and from about seven with letters. If a child is becoming short-sighted, we recommend myopia management spectacle lenses or contact lenses designed to slow the change, and a check every six months.

  • Exams free when you buy their glasses here
  • Polycarbonate lenses as standard
  • Free damage cover for one year
  • Spare pair at half price

Dry eye clinic

Dry, gritty or watery eyes are often caused by blocked oil glands along the eyelid rather than a lack of tears. We image the glands, measure how quickly your tear film breaks up and build a routine around warm compresses, lid hygiene and the right drops. Thursday is our dry eye clinic day.

  • Heated eye mask: $39
  • Lid hygiene kit: $18
  • In-clinic gland expression: $95
  • Review visit at 6 weeks included

Repairs, guarantees and the small print

Written plainly, so you know what happens if something goes wrong before it does.

  • Free for our patients

    Adjustments, screw replacement, new nose pads and ultrasonic cleaning, for as long as you own a frame from us. No appointment needed.

  • Frames from elsewhere

    We adjust any frame for free. New screws or nose pads are $10, hinge repairs from $25. Some soldered metal repairs go to a specialist and take a week.

  • Reglazing your own frame

    Bring a frame you love and we will fit new lenses into it. We check it first and tell you honestly if it will survive the glazing.

  • 60-day varifocal guarantee

    Wear new varifocals for at least two weeks. If you still cannot get on with them within 60 days, we remake them in a different design or switch them to single vision lenses at no charge.

  • 30-day prescription check

    If anything about a new prescription feels wrong, come back within 30 days and we will recheck your eyes for free and remake the lenses if needed.

  • 12-month frame warranty

    Manufacturing faults in frames, such as broken hinges or peeling plating, are covered for a year. Accidental damage is covered on care plans.

  • Cancelling an appointment

    Please give us 24 hours’ notice so we can offer the time to someone else. A second missed appointment without notice carries a $25 fee.

  • Running late

    We can usually still see you if you are up to 10 minutes late. After that we will offer the next free slot, often the same day.

  • Your prescription is yours

    You always leave with a written copy. There is no obligation to buy glasses or lenses from us.
