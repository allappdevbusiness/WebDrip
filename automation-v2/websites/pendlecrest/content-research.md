# Pendlecrest Watch Works: content research

Research for a fictional independent watch repair, servicing and restoration workshop. I researched facts and ideas only. No text, names, prices or layouts were copied from any business.

Note: the network proxy blocked direct page reads in this session (gearpatrol.com, ablogtowatch.com, bwcmg.org and en.wikipedia.org all returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`). So these notes come from 17 web searches and the result summaries. The links are the sources those searches surfaced.

## What workshops offer, and typical prices
- **Full service / overhaul (mechanical, automatic):** independents typically charge about $200–500 (£180–420), roughly 30–50% below brand service centres. Chronographs and complications cost about $350–850. UK price lists put a manual chronograph at ~£310 and an automatic chronograph at ~£350. ([5dwatches cost guide](https://5dwatches.com/blog/watch-service-cost-by-brand-2026), [authenticwrist](https://authenticwrist.com/guides/how-often-service-automatic-watch-cost-2026/), [mitka price list](https://mitka.co.uk/price-list/))
- **What a full service includes:** inspection, then complete disassembly with every part checked for wear, machine cleaning in specialist solutions, and reassembly with several different lubricants. Then regulation on a timing machine (a timegrapher measures rate, amplitude and beat error), new gaskets, a pressure test, and final quality control over several days. ([fratellowatches](https://www.fratellowatches.com/what-actually-hapens-during-a-regular-watch-service/), [watchesoflancashire](https://watchesoflancashire.com/services/servicing-a-mechanical-watch/), [fhinds](https://www.fhinds.co.uk/services/watch-servicing))
- **Parts:** a mainspring costs ~£14–50, and fitting a balance staff costs ~£45 plus the part. Hairspring and balance work is skilled and expensive.
- **Batteries:** the cell itself costs $2–12, and $20–30 fitted on a simple snap-back case. A screw-back case with a water test costs ~$38–70. Pressure tests cost $0–25 and gasket checks $10–40. ([battery guide](https://new.risingsunartscentre.org/news/watch-battery-replacement-cost-what-to-expect-2026.html))
- **Crystals:** acrylic costs $30–100, mineral $100–300 and sapphire $300+, depending on shape and water-resistance work. ([thepricer](https://www.thepricer.org/watch-crystal-replacement-cost/), [sangamon](https://sangamonwatches.com/pages/comprehensive-guide-to-watch-repair-costs-battery-replacements-overhauls-and-crystal-repairs))
- **Other services:** demagnetising (seconds on a demagnetiser, and it usually fixes a watch that suddenly gains minutes a day), bracelet sizing and link removal, strap fitting, quartz movement service or replacement, and case and bracelet refinishing.
- **Vintage and heirloom work:** pocket watches and mantel clocks. Aim to preserve original character, make or source period parts, re-time the balance. ([pocket watch restoration](https://geardiary.com/2009/06/30/restoring-family-treasures-my-great-grandfathers-pocket-watches/))

## How the job works (first contact to collection)
1. Drop off at the counter, or post the watch in using registered or insured post with plenty of padding. Some workshops send a padded postal box. ([grayandsons](https://www.grayandsons.com/repairs/process/ez-ship-box-for-watch-repair/), [jurawatches postal](https://www.jurawatches.co.uk/pages/watch-repairs))
2. The customer fills in an intake form (symptoms, history) and gets a receipt or ticket number.
3. Inspection, then a **written estimate before any work**. No chargeable work starts until the customer approves. Estimates are often free; some shops charge £25–50, refunded if the work goes ahead.
4. The work itself, often 2–6 weeks. Brand service centres can take 6 months or more. Customers like status updates and a ticket number to check progress. ([luxewatches FAQ](https://www.luxewatches.co.uk/faqs/category/servicing/), [repair-shop booking systems](https://ru.wordpress.org/plugins/computer-repair-shop/))
5. Testing: timing in several positions, a pressure test and a multi-day wear test.
6. Return by insured post or collection, with a **12-month guarantee** on the work (some offer 24 months on overhauls).

## What customers worry about and ask
- "How much will it cost?", "How long will it take?", "Is it worth servicing an old watch?"
- How often to service: mechanical watches every 3–5 years (some brands say 5–10). ([watchgecko](https://www.watchgecko.com/en-ca/blogs/magazine/when-should-you-service-your-watch))
- Signs a watch needs a service: losing or gaining minutes, a stiff or loose crown, moisture under the glass, grinding or rattling, a short power reserve. ([houseofwatches](https://www.houseofwatches.co.uk/blog/how-to-know-when-your-watch-needs-servicing))
- **Polishing vs keeping it original:** collectors value unpolished cases. Polishing removes metal and softens edges, and over-polishing can cut resale value by 5–20%. So workshops should ask first and offer a "light touch" option. ([luxewatches polishing](https://www.luxewatches.co.uk/does-polishing-affect-watch-value/), [Quill & Pad](https://quillandpad.com/2019/05/20/vintage-watch-restoration-should-you-or-not-a-guide-to-the-oft-controversial-world-of-making-things-worse-by-trying-to-make-them-better-reprise))
- **Trust signals:** a written quote before the movement is touched, photos of the disassembled movement on request, and clear test procedures (timing and pressure). Being open about methods, prices and turnaround also builds trust. Red flags: very low prices, being evasive, offering to open the case on the spot for a "quick fix". ([omegaforums thread](https://omegaforums.net/threads/how-to-evaluate-a-watchmaker.79918), [beckertime](https://beckertime.com/blog/the-importance-of-a-good-watchmaker/))
- Water resistance: 30 m means splashes only, 50–100 m means swimming, and 200 m+ means snorkelling and diving. Gaskets age, so pressure-test yearly if the watch gets wet. ([watch care guide](https://watchmaxx.com/watch-care-guide))
- Magnetism from phones, laptops, magnetic clasps and speakers. Suddenly gaining +2 to +10 minutes a day is a typical sign. ([watchscanning](https://www.watchscanning.com/guides/magnetized-watch-fix/))
- Care: wind manual and stopped automatics 20–40 turns, don't set the date between about 9pm and 3am, store the watch in a dry, padded box, and keep it away from magnets.

## What strong sites in this field include
- Clear price ranges by service type, often "from" prices with an estimate step.
- A step-by-step process, postal instructions and a printable checklist.
- An online estimate or booking request form, ticket and status tracking, and opening hours.
- A detailed FAQ, a care guide, the guarantee terms, and the watchmaker's background and training.
- Before/after restoration photos.

Then a marquee of the brands and calibres we service (generic movement types, no trademarks), symptoms with a "what's wrong with my watch?" checker, and services at a glance with filters. Also how a service works (pinned scroll story), a before/after restoration slider, a spotlight of the bench (looping loupe photo with pause/play), stats with count-ups (sample figures), and a sample review carousel with a decorative quote. Finally opening hours with an open-now indicator, the newsletter and a CTA.
- **services.html:** an animated header, then a services price table with tabs (servicing / repairs / restoration / quartz & small jobs), a service-tier comparison with a standard/premium toggle, and an estimate calculator (movement type, complications, crystal, extras). Also a turnaround estimator, a horizontal-scroll "inside a full service" walk-through, a parts glossary accordion and a care guide. Then the guarantee and policies, and an FAQ with search (14 questions).
- **book.html:** an animated header, then a choice between drop-off and postal with a booking picker (date and time slots), a quiz that recommends a service and a postal-kit checklist. Also a weekly bench timetable with day tabs, a restoration gallery (six cards) with a lightbox, the watchmaker team and a map illustration with directions tabs. Then a repair ticket tracker (demo reference) and the contact form with validation.
- Pricing in GBP. The address is invented ("14 Ropewalk Yard, Pendle Street"), as are the phone number and email.
