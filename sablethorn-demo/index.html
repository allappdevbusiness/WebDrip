02 — The process

Three fittings.
Eight weeks.

A bespoke suit is built slowly and in the open. You see it first as chalk on paper, then as loose white basting, then nearly finished. At each step we pin, re-chalk and re-cut until it moves the way you do.

  1. Week 1

    I. The conversation

    We talk about where you'll wear it, choose cloth together and take twenty-two measurements.

    About 60 minutes

  2. Week 2

    II. Your pattern

    Your cutter drafts a paper pattern by hand. It's filed under your name and kept for every future order.

    Kept for life

  3. Weeks 3–6

    III. Basted fittings

    Two fittings in loosely tacked cloth. We pull it apart and re-cut until the balance is right.

    Two visits, 45 min each

  4. Week 8

    IV. The finish

    Buttonholes, felling and a final press. You try it on once more and it goes home with you.

    Free pressing, forever
