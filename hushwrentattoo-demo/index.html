05 — Aftercare

Good questions.

Does fine line hurt more?+

Usually less. A single needle moves lightly, and we take breaks whenever you want one.

Will tiny lines blur over time?+

We size every design so the lines have room to settle. If it's too small to last, we'll tell you.

How do I look after it?+

You'll leave with a healing film, a printed care card and a check-in message on day three.

Are touch-ups included?+

Yes. One free touch-up within three months of your session.
