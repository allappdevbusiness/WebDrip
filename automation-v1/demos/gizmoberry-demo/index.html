How a session works

Ninety minutes, four happy steps.

  1. 1

    Warm-up puzzle

    A quick brain teaser to get everyone talking and thinking like engineers.

    10 MIN

  2. 2

    Build sprint

    Pairs grab a kit and build the week's robot, with coaches close by.

    40 MIN

  3. 3

    Test & tweak

    It wobbles? Great. We find out why, fix it, and try again together.

    25 MIN

  4. 4

    Show & tell

    Each pair demos their bot. Parents are welcome for the last ten minutes.

    15 MIN
