02 — The ritual

Three minutes, twice a day.

No twelve-step routine. Wash, press, seal. Your skin knows what to do with the rest.

i.60 sec

Wash

Massage a coin of Oat Milk Wash onto dry skin, then rinse with lukewarm water. Pat, don't rub.

ii.30 sec

Press

Three drops of Dew Serum in the morning, or Gold Root Oil at night. Press in with warm palms.

iii.60 sec

Seal

A pea of Cloud Cream over the top to hold it all in. Done. Go have your coffee.
