How it works

From first visit to front door

  1. 01Week 1

    Site visit

    We spend 90 minutes in your home, measure up and listen to how you really use it.

  2. 02Weeks 2–4

    Two options in card

    Two schemes, built as card models with sun studies, so you can compare them side by side.

  3. 03Weeks 5–16

    Planning & drawings

    We submit the application, answer the council and prepare drawings builders can price.

  4. 04On site

    Build & handover
