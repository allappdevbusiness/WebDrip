03 · A Saturday at Bobblefin

Our favourite morning of the week

  1. 8:30

    Splash Tots warm-up

    Bubbles, songs and the very first floats, grown-ups in the water too.

  2. 9:15

    Little Fins games

    Treasure dives, starfish floats and kicking races to the ducky line.

  3. 10:00

    Big Strokes squad

    Proper lengths, tumble turns and deep-end confidence.

  4. 11:00

    Badge parade

    Everyone who hit a first this week gets a cheer and a ducky badge.
